# .np domain needs dao registration

Date: 2026-07-28 13:10

---

A `.np` domain registration was rejected because the club isn't a registered
legal entity. To register a `.np` domain you must first register the
organisation at the **District Administration Office (DAO)** under the
**Association Registration Act 2034 (1977)**.

Full chain (DAO is step 3):

| Step | Where | What | ~Fee |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ward Office | Recommendation letter from the local ward | NPR 2,000 |
| 2 | DCC | District Coordination Committee recommendation (sometimes) | varies |
| 3 | DAO | Submit file → Registration Certificate | NPR 1,000 |
| 4 | IRD | PAN registration (within 30 days of DAO) | free |
| 5 | SWC | Social Welfare Council affiliation (within 3 months; needed for foreign funding) | NPR 3,000 |

Documents for step 3: DAO application form; constitution in Nepali (3 notarised
copies); signed founding-meeting minutes; list of 7+ founding members with
citizenship numbers; citizenship photocopies; police good-conduct reports for
office bearers; ward recommendation; office address proof.

Requirements: **min. 7 founding members** (Nepali citizens), a physical office
address, Nepali constitution. Total govt fees ~NPR 6,000; timeline ~2–3 months
Ward → SWC.
